## Brand & Style

This design system blends **High-Trust Corporate Modernism** with **High-Conversion Direct-Response E-Commerce**, tailored for the vibrant and fast-moving Nigerian retail landscape. It reflects a brand identity centered around discovery, prestige, and absolute transactional integrity. 

### Target Audience & Psychology
The primary customer base encompasses urban and semi-urban Nigerian consumers—smart shoppers across Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, and beyond—who seek uncommon, problem-solving lifestyle gadgets and trending innovations. Due to prevalent market skepticism regarding online order fulfillment, the visual language prioritizes immediate legitimacy, security reassurance (e.g., Pay on Delivery, money-back guarantees, verified reviews), and clarity of value proposition.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Structured Prestige:** Rich deep navy anchor tones evoke institutional permanence and dependability, balanced by refined metallic gold touches reminiscent of the brand emblem.
- **Conversion-Optimized Tactility:** Interactive elements carry satisfying mass, distinct borders, and responsive micro-elevations to guarantee intuitive tapping on mobile viewports.
- **Warm & Welcoming Clarity:** Backed by clean ivory creams and pure white cards, reducing visual friction while spotlighting rich product photography, urgency badges, and bundle incentives.

## Colors

The color system is organized to establish institutional credibility, highlight exclusive deal mechanics, and facilitate rapid decision-making across high-intent checkout funnels.

### Core Roles
- **Primary Navy (`#0E294B` / `#0B1E36`):** The foundational anchor. Applied to dominant navigational banners, primary buttons, structural headlines, and footer seals. It establishes authority and reliability.
- **Brand Gold Accent (`#D4AF37` / `#C59A27`):** The prestige signifier. Used intentionally for star ratings, premium member badges, VIP flash-deal tags, and accent borders on featured tier bundles.
- **Trust & Verification Emerald (`#10B981`):** Functional confirmation color. Applied to "Payment on Delivery Available," "In Stock - Ready to Ship," "Verified Buyer" checks, and delivery timeline chips.
- **Urgency Coral & Amber (`#E11D48` / `#D97706`):** High-energy attention driver. Strictly reserved for countdown timer clocks, limited-stock thresholds, discount percentages ("Save ₦8,500"), and deal expiration indicators.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - `Surface Main`: Crisp Warm Cream (`#FBF9F5`) to soften eye strain during prolonged catalog browsing.
  - `Surface Container`: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for product cards, bottom sheets, and checkout modules.
  - `Neutral Dark`: Slate Charcoal (`#1A202C`) for hyper-legible body typography and icon fills.
  - `Neutral Border`: Muted Bone Gray (`#E7E2D8`) for crisp separation lines without visual clutter.

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances character and absolute legibility.

- **Display & Headings (Syne):** Syne introduces architectural angles, sculptural curves, and distinctive flair to main category banners, product titles, and campaign promotional headlines. Its geometric balance mirrors the interlocking lines of the brand emblem.
- **Body & Numerical Pricing (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Designed with wide open apertures and tall x-heights, ensuring effortless readability on budget and flagship smartphone displays under strong outdoor sunlight.
- **Naira Currency Representation (₦):** Figures and currency symbols must be rendered in `Plus Jakarta Sans` with bold/extrabold weights (`800`) to guarantee unmistakable numerical clarity, preventing confusion between crossed zeros, commas, and currency signs during fast checkout decisions.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first fluid layout framework engineered for quick handheld one-thumb navigation.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Bounds
- **Mobile (Base to 639px):** 4-column layout, `16px` (`1rem`) outer page margin, `16px` gutters. Two-column vertical product card grids with sticky utility anchors at the bottom edge.
- **Tablet (640px to 1023px):** 8-column layout, `24px` outer margins, `20px` gutters. Adapts listing grids into 3 columns; converts checkout into split-pane inspection modals.
- **Desktop (1024px and up):** 12-column layout centered within a max-width container of `1280px`. `32px` page margins, `24px` gutters. Product detail pages transition into dual-column views (sticky gallery on left, sticky purchase accordion on right).

### Mobile Vertical Rhythm
Touch targets maintain a minimum dimension of `48px × 48px`. Compact utility paddings (`space-sm` to `space-md`) within product grids ensure critical details (discount badge, stock counter, delivery speed) appear above the scroll fold on typical mobile viewports.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a hybrid system of crisp structural borders, surface warmth transitions, and directional ambient shadows to convey physical layers.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#FBF9F5` base background.
- **Level 1 (Default Containers & Cards):** Pure White `#FFFFFF` with a hairline stroke (`1px solid #ECE7DE`) and soft resting shadow: `0px 2px 8px -1px rgba(14, 41, 75, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Featured Bundle Deals & Interactive Dropdowns):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an accented border (`1.5px solid #D4AF37`) or elevated shadow: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(14, 41, 75, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Purchase Bar & Mobile Menus):** Docked elements pinned to bottom or top edges carry high-deflection blur: `0px -4px 20px 0px rgba(14, 41, 75, 0.12)`, backed by `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
- **Level 4 (Transactional Modals & Direct WhatsApp Floating Trigger):** Floating dynamic objects cast a deep ambient shadow: `0px 16px 36px -6px rgba(11, 30, 54, 0.22)`.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates a modern, friendly `rounded-2xl` contouring standard (`roundedness: 2`), ensuring interactive components feel approachable, modern, and ergonomic for tactile mobile interaction.

### Corner Radius Mapping
- **Product Cards, Offer Banners & Review Blocks:** `1rem` (`16px`) standard corner radius. Provides a smooth card-deck feel on phone screens.
- **Primary Buttons & Sticky Bar Triggers:** `0.75rem` (`12px`) to preserve structural stability and solid rectangular presence under high-urgency call-to-actions.
- **Badges, Deal Pills & Notification Tags:** `9999px` (Full Pill) for distinction against rectangular product boundaries.
- **Input Fields & Dropdown Trigger Menus:** `0.75rem` (`12px`) with gentle inner padding to create a structured entry field.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Order Now - Pay on Delivery"):** Background Deep Navy (`#0E294B`), text Crisp White, bold font. Enhanced with a subtle golden glow or dual-tone top border (`2px solid #D4AF37`) to emphasize exclusivity. Height: `52px` minimum on mobile.
- **High-Urgency Action Button ("Claim Deal Before Timer Ends"):** Deep Navy background with an animated Gold accent ring or high-contrast Coral badge counter.
- **Secondary / WhatsApp Direct Action:** Background Emerald Green (`#10B981`), Crisp White text with WhatsApp vector icon. Instantly connects customers to verified sales agents.
- **Outline Button:** Border `1.5px solid #0E294B`, background transparent, text `#0E294B`.

### Trust Badges & Guarantee Seals
- **Payment on Delivery Pill:** Pill-shaped badge featuring an Emerald fill (`#10B981` at 12% opacity), dark green label text, and a verified shield icon.
- **Lagos / Abuja Fast Shipping Tag:** Subtle slate blue background, highlighting delivery estimates (e.g., "⚡ Arrives Tomorrow in Lagos").
- **100% Money-Back Seal:** Crisp circular or rounded card chip with Gold metallic accent stroke (`#D4AF37`) and star rating indicators.

### Product & Bundle Cards
- **Standard Product Card:** White container, `16px` border-radius, clean image slot with a floating discount tag top-left ("-42% OFF") and bookmark icon top-right. Displays product title, star rating, current price (`₦XX,XXX`) in bold, strikethrough original price, and direct "Add to Cart" tap target.
- **Tiered Bundle Selector (Buy 2 Get 1 Free / Save 20%):** Outlined selection card with radio button on the left. The selected tier activates a solid Gold boundary (`#D4AF37`) with a "Most Popular" or "Best Value" chip pinned to the top border.

### Sticky Mobile Purchase Dock
- Pinned to the bottom viewport edge on all mobile product detail pages.
- Left segment: Dynamic price tally (`₦ Total`) with free delivery micro-label.
- Right segment: Large primary button ("Complete Order Now") that triggers an instant one-page slide-up checkout modal to eliminate multi-step cart abandonment.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E7E2D8`, focused state transitions to `#0E294B` with an outer focus ring (`rgba(14, 41, 75, 0.15)`).
- Input labels use `label-md` in bold charcoal. Preset phone number inputs prepend the Nigerian standard flag and dial code (`+234`).

### Countdown Urgency Modules
- Dark navy surface strip with Coral numbers (`#E11D48`). Segmented flip-boxes for Hours, Minutes, and Seconds with uppercase micro-labels to drive timely checkout completion.
